# Photography reset — 16 October 2026

## Evidence and limits

Adobe's contributor guidance explicitly identifies food, families, health and wellness, nature/wildlife and business as evergreen selling topics. It recommends realistic everyday settings, natural interactions, clear buyer applications, and a mixture of evergreen and seasonal work:
https://stock.adobe.com/pages/artisthub/get-started/create-stock-content-that-sells-stock-contributor-guide-pt-1

Adobe's call for authentic people asks for varied ages, bodies, backgrounds and abilities, realistic places and attainable interiors:
https://stock.adobe.com/pages/artisthub/get-inspired/no-filter-authentic-stock

The current curated collection index includes mid-life wellness, multigenerational workplaces, analog activities, accessibility and sustainability:
https://stock.adobe.com/ie/collections

These are Adobe's demand guidance and editorial selections, not verified image-level sales rankings. The requested contributor URL with order=nb_downloads could not be retrieved in this research session. No claim is made to have inspected 50/100 portfolios or to know download totals. The supplied dashboard screenshots are user-provided examples, not a representative market sample.

## Practical shot priorities

| Direction | Literal photograph | Buyer and placement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food preparation | Cook folding dumplings at a kitchen counter | Recipe publisher, cooking class advertisement |
| Family care | Parent fastening a child's raincoat at the front door | Family brand, rainy-season campaign |
| Healthcare | Physical therapist guiding an older adult's seated exercise | Clinic website, mobility education |
| Small business | Shop owner packing an online order at a worktable | Shipping service, business article |
| Accessible everyday life | Wheelchair user approaching a step-free train platform | Transport service, accessibility information |
| Pet care | Veterinarian examining a calm dog's paw | Veterinary booking page |
| Interiors | Bright compact kitchen showing practical vertical storage | Home renovation article |
| Local activities | Neighbors participating in a pottery workshop | Community program announcement |

These are candidates to test, not promises of sales. Differentiate through a specific useful action, credible place, framing, lighting and accurate detail. Do not reconstruct another contributor's composition or signature style.

## Production decisions

- JPEG photographs only. PNG and the 18-field material-concept planner are retired.
- Use reviewed monthly subjects literally; no conversion into sculptures, miniatures, metaphors or object clusters.
- Planning is local and makes zero billed API calls.
- Old browser requests and PNG requests are blocked before image generation.
- Existing historical files remain downloadable.
- Image QA remains bounded by the existing three-replacement limit; rejected images at that limit retain their warning and reasons.
- No images were generated or regeneration endpoints invoked during this reset.
