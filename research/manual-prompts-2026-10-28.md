# Manual stock research and prompt plan — 28 October 2026

Manual Chrome observations; search was sorted by relevance. Results and counts are not evidence of sales or demand. Images are to be original and must not imitate individual stock assets. Do not depict real news events. All prompts: JPEG, full bleed, no visible faces, no text, numbers, logos, watermark, artificial borders, or brands. Copy space must be part of the natural scene. Screens may show simple non-text visual shapes only, never pseudo-text. These are proposals; they have not been generated or added to the automatic pipeline.

## Observed searches

- [Online privacy](https://stock.adobe.com/search?k=online%20privacy): prominent locks/shields, handheld phones, laptop users, warnings and account-protection visuals. Differentiate through relatable specific actions instead of another floating padlock.
- [Smart home display](https://stock.adobe.com/search?k=smart%20home%20display): wall-mounted controllers, tablets and smart thermostats in bright modern interiors; a hand interacting clarifies purpose. Avoid mimicking brands or creating illegible UI.
- [Light pollution](https://stock.adobe.com/search?k=light%20pollution): city glow and stars, night streets, shielded lights, measurement and ecological effects; the broad query also returns unrelated material. Make cause or solution visibly clear.
