Topic angle: everyday health and household sustainability habits, drawn from the [Bain Consumer Lab ESG Survey 2026](https://www.bain.com/insights/for-consumers-sustainability-isnt-gone-but-it-is-changing-ceo-sustainability-report-2026/) and [PwC Voice of the Consumer 2026](https://www.pwc.com/id/votc-2026-act). `medicine expiry date checking` passed the count but was dropped because the subject needs printed dates.
