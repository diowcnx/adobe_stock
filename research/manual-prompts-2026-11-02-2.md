Keywords and result counts are in `keyword-competition.csv`. Selected terms had 20–500 results; competition is not evidence of sales. Topic angle: Thai crafts, farm produce and small business, an area with few strong assets. First round to study the top 3–5 Adobe results per keyword: each brief's `Top results` line records what buyers see first, and the prompt fills a gap instead of copying any image. Passing terms left out: `sticky rice bamboo steamer basket kitchen` (overlaps the basket weaving brief) and `rubber sheets drying bamboo rack` (22 results, weak buyer use). Images must be original and must not imitate individual stock assets. All prompts: JPEG, full bleed, no visible faces, no text, numbers, logos, watermark, artificial borders or brands.
